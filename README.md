
# Civica

**Civica** is a modular SaaS platform designed to centralize and simplify business operations. It provides a growing suite of tools—such as **Inventory**, **Management**, **Referrals**, **Tracking**, and more—allowing organizations to select, combine, and customize the modules they need.  

Whether you're a startup looking for agility or an enterprise managing complex workflows, Civica adapts to your needs with its flexible, plug-and-play architecture.

---

## Key Features

- **Modular Architecture** – Choose only the tools you need.  
- **Unified Platform** – Eliminate silos with seamless integration.  
- **Scalable & Extensible** – Start small, scale as you grow.  
- **Secure & Reliable** – Enterprise-grade security and monitoring.  
- **Cross-Platform** – Consistent experience on desktop and mobile.  

---

## Available Modules

- **Inventory** – Manage stock, supplies, and resources with real-time updates.  
- **Management** – Tools for project planning, scheduling, and team coordination.  
- **Referrals** – Track and manage referral programs and partnerships.  
- **Tracking** – Monitor KPIs, performance, and activity across your organization.  
- **(More coming soon)** – Continuous expansion with new modules.  

---

## Getting Started

1. **Sign Up** – Create your Civica account at civica.io.  
2. **Select Modules** – Activate the modules you need.  
3. **Configure** – Customize each module’s settings.  
4. **Collaborate** – Invite team members and assign roles.
